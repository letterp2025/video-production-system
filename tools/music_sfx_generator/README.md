# Music and SFX Generator Options

**Status:** Deferred. No generator has been selected or installed.

The current practical direction is to look for suitable existing music and sound effects first, record their source and license, and test them in an edit. Building a synthesis workflow would add composition, audition, revision, arrangement, and mixing work.

## Possible future paths

- **Notation-first piano sketch:** Draft a short melody or chord progression in text notation, then audition and revise it in MuseScore Studio.
- **Procedural sound effects:** rFXGen may suit short, deliberately game-like interface cues.
- **Code-based music:** Sonic Pi or Csound could be explored if a real production repeatedly needs original cues that licensed sources cannot supply.

These are candidates, not a selected production system. No audio-generation workflow has been tested here.

## If revisited

Start with one clearly defined cue and a short duration. Compare the time needed to make and revise it with finding an existing licensed asset. Keep source notation or sound recipes with the video project, document the tool and asset terms, and review the final mix by listening.
